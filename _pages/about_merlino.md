## [Giovanni Merlino](https://unifind.unime.it/get/person/121567)

_Lab Director · Associate Professor_

Giovanni Merlino is Associate Professor of Computer Science Engineering at the Department of Engineering of the University of Messina, where he leads the ProSoDiAC Lab.

His research focuses on software-defined IoT and cyber-physical systems, with particular emphasis on IT/OT convergence and decentralization protocols.

He conceived the [I/Ocloud](https://shop.elsevier.com/books/assembling-smart-cyber-physical-systems/benomar/978-0-443-29837-0) paradigm. His doctoral research led to the design and implementation of the [Stack4Things](https://github.com/AssemblingSmartCPS/ch13) middleware, the main software artifact of his PhD thesis, providing a foundation for his subsequent work on software-defined IoT and cyber-physical systems.

He also co-founded [SmartMe.io](https://smartme.io), an academic spin-off.
